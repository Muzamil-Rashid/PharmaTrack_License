What it does

Built for the pharmacy counter, not a distribution warehouse

PharmaTrack is single-location retail and clinic software — everything a pharmacist or clinic owner needs to run day-to-day operations, nothing built for multi-branch distribution.

Inventory & Stock Tracking
Track every item by batch and expiry, with low-stock warnings before you run out at the counter.
Billing & Sales
Fast, prescription-linked billing at the counter — built for a busy shop, not a back office.
Expiry & Batch Alerts
Get flagged automatically before stock goes past its shelf life, batch by batch.
Patient & Customer Records
Keep purchase and prescription history on hand for every repeat customer.
Reports & Insights
Daily sales, stock valuation, and movement reports whenever you need them.
Works Fully Offline
No internet connection required — your data lives on your own machine, always.

How to install & run:

2. Right-click the downloaded zip file and extract it.
3. Once extracted, a win-unpacked folder will be created. Open it.
4. Double-click PharmaTrack.exe to start the software.
